# Code of Conduct

Rick Industries Inc. expects professional, respectful, and constructive collaboration.

## Standards

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.
- Credit contributors and document important decisions.
- Raise risks early and communicate blockers honestly.
- Keep work aligned with the approved scope.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information, and abusive conduct are not accepted.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
