# Rick Industries Inc.

**Engineering products with discipline, focus, and continuity.**

Rick Industries Inc. is Rick's personal engineering company and product ecosystem.

## Operating model

- **Professional Services** — engineering and QA work that sustains the ecosystem.
- **The Foundry** — one focused product MVP at a time.
- **Engineering** — internal platforms, infrastructure, and developer experience.
- **Knowledge** — reusable technical decisions and documentation.
- **Business** — strategy, operations, and sustainable growth.

## Active product

### Project Horizon

A personal financial advisor for Latin America that helps people understand income, expenses, available balance, and whether a purchase is financially viable.

## Execution principle

> FOCO MVP. One day at a time. One commit at a time. One user at a time.

Built with discipline, not inspiration.
